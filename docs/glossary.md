## A

### Accuracy
The proportion of correct predictions made by an AI model.

*Example: A donor retention model with 85% accuracy correctly predicts whether 85 out of 100 donors will give again.*

### AI Agent
An autonomous AI system that can plan and execute complex tasks.

*Example: An AI agent that monitors your social media, identifies engagement opportunities, drafts responses, and schedules posts.*

### Algorithmic Bias
Systematic errors in AI systems that create unfair outcomes for certain groups.

*Example: A grant recommendation system trained primarily on data from large urban non-profits might unfairly score rural organizations lower.*

### Artificial Intelligence (AI)
Computer systems capable of performing tasks requiring human intelligence.

*Example: A chatbot that answers donor questions 24/7 without human intervention.*

---

## C

### Chatbot
AI-powered conversational assistant providing automated responses.

*Example: A chatbot on your website answering FAQs about volunteer opportunities, donation methods, and program schedules.*

### Classification
Task of categorizing data into predefined classes.

*Example: Automatically categorizing support emails as "donations," "volunteer inquiries," or "program questions."*

### CRM (Constituent Relationship Management)
Database managing relationships with supporters.

*Example: Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud tracking donor history, volunteer hours, and event attendance.*

---

## D

### Data Privacy
Protection of personal information from unauthorized access.

*Example: Encrypting beneficiary data and limiting staff access based on role.*

### Deep Learning
Machine learning using neural networks with multiple layers.

*Example: Image recognition identifying people in event photos for automatic tagging.*

---

## G

### Generative AI
AI systems creating new content (text, images, audio, video).

*Example: Using ChatGPT to draft newsletter content or DALL-E to create event graphics.*

### GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
European data protection regulation.

*Example: Ensuring EU donors can request deletion of their data from your system.*

---

## L

### Large Language Model (LLM)
AI model trained on vast text data to understand and generate language.

*Example: GPT-4, Claude, or Gemini powering your grant writing assistance tool.*

---

## M

### Machine Learning (ML)
Systems learning from data without explicit programming.

*Example: Analyzing past donation patterns to predict which current donors are likely to increase their giving.*

---

## N

### Natural Language Processing (NLP)
AI's ability to understand and generate human language.

*Example: Automatically translating your website content into Spanish, Hmong, and Vietnamese.*

### Neural Network
Computing system inspired by biological neural networks.

*Example: The underlying architecture enabling facial recognition or voice transcription.*

---

## P

### Predictive Analytics
Using data to forecast future outcomes.

*Example: Predicting which volunteers are at risk of disengaging so you can intervene early.*

### Prompt
Input text given to an AI model to generate a response.

*Example: "Write a 200-word thank-you email for a first-time $100 donor to our youth program."*

### Prompt Engineering
Crafting effective prompts to get desired AI outputs.

*Example: Adding "Be warm but professional, mention specific program impact" to improve output quality.*

---

## R

### ROI (Return on Investment)
Measuring financial value of AI initiatives.

*Example: Calculating that your $5,000 AI chatbot investment saved 200 staff hours worth $10,000.*

---

## S

### Sentiment Analysis
Determining emotional tone in feedback or communications.

*Example: Analyzing program evaluation surveys to identify patterns in participant satisfaction.*

---

## T

### Token
Basic unit of text processed by language models.

*Example: The sentence "AI helps non-profits" contains roughly 5 tokens.*

### Training Data
Dataset used to teach an AI model.

*Example: Using 5 years of donation records to train a model predicting donor retention.*

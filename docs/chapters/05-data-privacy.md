# Chapter 5: Data Privacy and Security

Protecting personal information while leveraging AI.

**Learning Objectives:** Understand privacy regulations, implement data protection, ensure compliance

**Time:** 3-4 hours

---

## 5.1 Why Privacy Matters for Non-Profits

Your community members trust you with sensitive information. One privacy breach can:
- Destroy years of trust
- Lead to legal consequences
- Harm vulnerable populations
- End your organization

**Key Regulations:**
- GDPR (European Union)
- CCPA (California)
- HIPAA (Health data)
- FERPA (Education data)

---

## 5.2 Personal Identifiable Information (PII)

**Direct PII:** Name, SSN, email, phone, address  
**Indirect PII:** Demographics that could identify someone when combined

**Protected Classes:**
- Health information
- Financial data
- Immigration status
- Children's data (COPPA)

---

## 5.3 Data Minimization

**Principle:** Collect only what you actually need.

**Questions to ask:**
- Do we really need this data?
- How long must we keep it?
- Who needs access?
- What's the risk if it's exposed?

---

## 5.4 AI-Specific Privacy Concerns

1. **Training Data:** Personal info in AI training sets
2. **Inference Attacks:** AI reveals training data
3. **Model Inversion:** Reconstruct individual records
4. **Membership Inference:** Determine if someone's data was used
5. **Third-Party Risk:** Cloud AI services handling your data

---

## 5.5 Best Practices

✅ **Anonymization** - Remove identifiers
✅ **Encryption** - Protect data at rest and in transit
✅ **Access Controls** - Limit who sees what
✅ **Consent Management** - Clear opt-in/opt-out
✅ **Regular Audits** - Check compliance
✅ **Incident Response Plan** - Prepare for breaches
✅ **Privacy by Design** - Build in from start

---

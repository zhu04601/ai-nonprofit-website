# Chapter 13: Organizational AI Strategy and Roadmap

Building a sustainable AI future.

**Learning Objectives:** Develop strategy, create roadmaps, plan for sustainability

**Time:** 4-5 hours

---

## 13.1 Strategic AI Vision

### Vision Statement Elements

**Components:**
1. Where you are now (current state)
2. Where you want to be (future state)
3. Why AI matters to your mission
4. How AI aligns with values
5. Timeline (3-5 years)

**Example:**
```
"By 2028, [Organization] will leverage AI to serve 50% more 
families while maintaining personalized, culturally responsive 
care. AI will handle routine tasks, freeing staff to focus on 
relationship-building and complex case management. We will be 
recognized as a leader in ethical, equitable AI use in the 
non-profit sector."
```

---

## 13.2 Strategic Priorities

### Prioritization Framework

**Impact vs. Effort Matrix:**

```
High Impact, Low Effort → DO FIRST (Quick Wins)
High Impact, High Effort → PLAN CAREFULLY (Major Projects)
Low Impact, Low Effort → DO IF TIME (Nice to Have)
Low Impact, High Effort → DON'T DO (Avoid)
```

### Common Priorities

**Year 1: Foundation**
- Build staff AI literacy
- Implement 2-3 quick wins
- Establish governance
- Clean data systems

**Year 2: Expansion**
- Scale successful pilots
- Integrate tools
- Measure ROI
- Share learnings

**Year 3: Optimization**
- Advanced applications
- Custom solutions
- Lead sector innovation
- Mentor other organizations

## 13.3 Resource Allocation

### Budget Considerations

**Technology (40%):**
- Software subscriptions
- Infrastructure
- Tools and platforms

**People (35%):**
- Training
- Staff time
- Consulting/expertise

**Change Management (15%):**
- Communication
- Documentation
- Process redesign

**Contingency (10%):**
- Unexpected costs
- Opportunities
- Course corrections

---

## 13.4 Risk Management

### Common Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| **Staff resistance** | Early involvement, training, communication |
| **Technical failure** | Pilots, backups, vendor SLAs |
| **Budget overruns** | Phased approach, contingency funds |
| **Data breach** | Security audits, encryption, access controls |
| **Vendor lock-in** | Data portability, open standards |
| **Mission drift** | Regular alignment checks, ethics review |

---

## 13.5 Roadmap Development

### 3-Year Roadmap Template

**Year 1: Foundation**
- Q1: Assess, plan, secure buy-in
- Q2: Launch 2 pilots
- Q3: Evaluate, train staff
- Q4: Scale successes

**Year 2: Growth**
- Q1: Integrate tools
- Q2: Expand to new areas
- Q3: Measure ROI
- Q4: Plan year 3

**Year 3: Maturity**
- Q1: Optimize systems
- Q2: Advanced features
- Q3: Share knowledge
- Q4: Update strategy

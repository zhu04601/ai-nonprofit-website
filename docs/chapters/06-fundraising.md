# Chapter 6: AI for Fundraising and Development

Transforming donor relationships with intelligent tools.

**Learning Objectives:** Implement AI-powered fundraising, donor analytics, campaign optimization

**Time:** 4-5 hours

---

## 6.1 The Fundraising Challenge

Non-profits face:
- Donor acquisition costs rising
- Retention rates declining (avg 45%)
- Competition for attention intense
- Limited development staff
- Need for personalization at scale

**AI Solution:** Automate routine tasks, personalize at scale, predict giving behavior.

---

## 6.2 Donor Management Systems with AI

Modern CRMs with AI features:
- **Bloomerang** - Retention focus
- **Salesforce Non-Profit Cloud** - Enterprise
- **DonorPerfect** - Mid-size orgs
- **Little Green Light** - Small orgs

**AI Features:**
- Predictive giving scores
- Automated segmentation
- Personalization at scale
- Optimal contact timing
- Churn prediction

---

## 6.3 Donor Segmentation

**Traditional:** Manual grouping by donation amount  
**AI-Powered:** Automatic clustering by behavior patterns

**Key Segments:**
1. Major donors (capacity + interest)
2. Mid-level prospects (upgrade potential)
3. Loyal small donors (consistency)
4. Lapsed donors (re-engagement)
5. First-time donors (retention focus)

---

## 6.4 Predictive Giving

**What it predicts:**
- Likelihood to give again
- Optimal ask amount
- Best channel (email, phone, mail)
- Ideal timing
- Interest areas

**How it works:**
- Analyzes past giving patterns
- Considers external factors
- Updates predictions continuously
- Scores all donors

---

## 6.5 Email Optimization

**AI helps with:**
- Subject line generation (A/B testing)
- Send time optimization
- Content personalization
- Segment targeting
- Performance prediction

**Example:**
```
Traditional: "Support Our Mission" at 10am Tuesday to everyone
AI-Optimized: Personalized subjects sent at optimal times per donor
Result: 40% higher open rates, 25% more donations
```

---

## 6.6 Grant Writing Assistance

**AI can:**
- Draft initial content
- Research foundation priorities
- Match your programs to funders
- Format proposals
- Generate required documents

**AI cannot:**
- Build genuine relationships
- Understand your unique story
- Replace strategic thinking
- Guarantee funding

**Best Practice:** AI drafts, humans refine and personalize.

---

## 6.7 Real-World Success

**Case: Regional Food Bank**
- Implemented predictive giving model
- Identified 200 upgrade prospects
- Personalized outreach campaigns
- Result: $180K additional revenue, 32% increase

**Key Factors:**
- Clean historical data (5 years)
- Strong human follow-up
- Gradual rollout and testing
- Staff buy-in through training

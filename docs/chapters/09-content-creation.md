Creating compelling content at scale.

**Learning Objectives:** Leverage AI for writing, design, social media, and brand consistency

**Time:** 3-4 hours

## 9.1 The Content Challenge

Non-profits need constant content:
- Social media posts (daily)
- Email newsletters (weekly)
- Blog articles (monthly)
- Grant proposals (ongoing)
- Annual reports
- Marketing materials

**Reality:** Limited staff, limited time, unlimited needs

**AI Solution:** Draft, edit, and optimize content faster

## 9.3 Brand Voice Consistency

### Training AI on Your Voice

**Steps:**
1. Collect 10-20 examples of your best content
2. Identify voice characteristics (warm, professional, inspiring)
3. Create style guide
4. Use examples in prompts
5. Iterate until consistent

**Example Prompt:**
```
Write like this organization:
[paste 3 sample posts]

Key traits:
- Warm and inclusive
- Focus on community impact
- Use storytelling
- Avoid jargon
- Celebrate diversity

Now write a post about our volunteer program.
```

## 9.4 AI Image Generation

### Tools

- **DALL-E** (OpenAI) - High quality, versatile
- **Midjourney** - Artistic, creative
- **Stable Diffusion** - Open source
- **Adobe Firefly** - Commercial safe
- **Canva AI** - Integrated design

### Use Cases

**Social Media Graphics:**
- Event announcements
- Quote cards
- Infographics
- Campaign visuals

**Marketing Materials:**
- Flyer backgrounds
- Newsletter headers
- Website images
- Presentation slides

### Best Practices

✅ Use for backgrounds and abstract concepts  
✅ Combine with real photos for authenticity  
✅ Always review for appropriateness  
✅ Check licensing terms  
✅ Disclose AI-generated images  

❌ Don't use for people (uncanny valley)  
❌ Avoid for sensitive topics  
❌ Don't misrepresent as real photos

## 9.7 Practical Workflow

### Weekly Content Creation

**Monday: Plan**
- AI suggests content themes
- Review analytics for what worked
- Create content calendar

**Tuesday-Thursday: Create**
- AI drafts posts
- Human edits and personalizes
- Design graphics with AI assistance
- Schedule posts

**Friday: Engage**
- Monitor comments
- AI suggests responses
- Thank engagers
- Share user content

# Chapter 15: AI Governance and the Future

Ensuring responsible, sustainable AI use.

**Learning Objectives:** Establish governance, prepare for the future, maintain leadership

**Time:** 4 hours

---

## 15.1 AI Governance Framework

### Why Governance Matters

**Without governance:**
- Inconsistent practices
- Ethical violations
- Security breaches
- Mission drift
- Wasted resources

**With governance:**
- Clear accountability
- Consistent standards
- Risk management
- Stakeholder trust
- Strategic alignment

---

## 15.2 Governance Structure

### Roles and Responsibilities

**AI Steering Committee:**
- **Composition:** Executive director, program leads, IT, community members
- **Responsibilities:** Strategy, major decisions, resource allocation
- **Frequency:** Quarterly

**AI Ethics Board:**
- **Composition:** Diverse stakeholders, external experts
- **Responsibilities:** Ethics review, bias audits, community concerns
- **Frequency:** Bi-annual + as needed

**AI Operations Lead:**
- **Role:** Day-to-day AI management
- **Responsibilities:** Implementation, training, monitoring
- **Reports to:** Executive team

---

## 15.3 Policy Development

### Essential Policies

**1. AI Use Policy**
- When AI is appropriate
- Approval processes
- Human oversight requirements
- Prohibited uses

**2. Data Privacy Policy**
- What data is collected
- How it's protected
- Who has access
- Retention periods

**3. Ethics Policy**
- Core values and principles
- Bias testing requirements
- Community input process
- Complaint procedures

**4. Procurement Policy**
- Vendor evaluation criteria
- Security requirements
- Contract terms
- Exit strategies

## 15.4 The Future of AI

### Emerging Trends (2026-2030)

**1. Multimodal AI**
- Combines text, image, audio, video
- More natural interactions
- Better accessibility
- Richer content

**2. Personalization at Scale**
- True 1:1 communication
- Adaptive learning paths
- Individualized services
- While preserving privacy

**3. Autonomous Agents**
- AI systems that plan and execute
- End-to-end task completion
- Reduced human intervention
- Greater efficiency

**4. Smaller, Specialized Models**
- Custom AI for specific tasks
- Lower costs
- Faster performance
- Better privacy

**5. Improved Explainability**
- Understand AI decisions
- Greater transparency
- Easier auditing
- Increased trust

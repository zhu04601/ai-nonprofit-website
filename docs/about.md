# About This Course

## Overview

**AI Integration for Non-Profit Organizations** is a comprehensive, pedagogically-designed textbook developed specifically for mission-driven organizations. Created in partnership with Asian Media Access, this course bridges the gap between cutting-edge AI technology and community-centered non-profit work.

## Development Philosophy

### Mission-First Approach
Every aspect of this course prioritizes organizational mission over technological capability. We believe AI should serve people and communities, not the other way around.

### Equity & Inclusion
Special attention to serving marginalized communities, multilingual populations, and organizations with limited resources.

### Practical & Actionable
Real tools, real budgets, real constraints. No theoretical concepts without practical application.

### Ethics at the Core
Responsible AI isn't an add-on — it's integrated into every chapter, every example, every recommendation.

## Pedagogical Design

This course follows evidence-based instructional design principles:

* **Bloom's Taxonomy**: Systematic cognitive development from knowledge to creation
* **Scaffolded Learning**: Progressive complexity with clear prerequisites
* **Active Learning**: Hands-on MicroSims and projects, not just reading
* **Mixed Media**: Text, visuals, interactives, and assessments
* **Spaced Repetition**: Concepts revisited and reinforced throughout

## Course Statistics

## About Asian Media Access

[Asian Media Access](https://ww1.amamedia.org/) is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization based in St. Paul, Minnesota, dedicated to connecting the disconnected through multimedia arts, technology, and community organizing.

### AMA's Programs

* **e-Magine Initiative**: Training in AI, generative AI, and digital marketing
* **Multimedia Education**: Youth and community media programs
* **Community Organizing**: Advocacy and civic engagement
* **Cultural Events**: Celebrating AAPI heritage and culture
* **Capacity Building**: Supporting community organizations

### Why AMA?

This textbook embodies AMA's commitment to:

* Bridging the digital divide
* Empowering underserved communities
* Making technology accessible and practical
* Maintaining cultural connections while adopting innovation
* Building sustainable organizational capacity

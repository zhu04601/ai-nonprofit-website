Answers to 100+ common questions about AI for non-profits.

## Getting Started

### Q: We're a small non-profit with a $200K budget. Is AI too expensive for us?

**A:** No! Many AI tools offer free tiers or non-profit discounts. Start with free tools like ChatGPT, Google's Gemini, or Claude for content creation. Many CRM systems include AI features at no extra cost. Your initial investment might be just staff time for learning and setup.

### Q: Do we need technical staff to use AI?

**A:** Not necessarily. Many modern AI tools are designed for non-technical users with intuitive interfaces. However, having at least one staff member who's comfortable learning new technology is helpful. Consider partnering with organizations like Asian Media Access that offer training programs.

### Q: How long does AI implementation take?

**A:** It varies widely. Simple tools (like using ChatGPT for content creation) can be adopted in days. Comprehensive CRM implementation with AI features might take 3-6 months. Our recommendation: Start small with quick wins, then expand gradually.

## Implementation

### Q: What if our staff resist using AI?

**A:** Resistance is normal! Address it through:

- Clear communication about how AI supports (not replaces) staff
- Training and ongoing support
- Celebrating early wins
- Involving staff in selection and implementation
- Starting with tools that make their jobs easier

### Q: How do we measure if AI is working?

**A:** Define success metrics before implementation. Examples:

- Time saved on repetitive tasks
- Increased donor retention rates
- More program participants reached
- Cost per outcome delivered
- Staff satisfaction scores

## Ethics & Privacy

### Q: How do we ensure AI doesn't harm the communities we serve?

**A:** Follow these principles:

- Always prioritize community trust over efficiency
- Be transparent about AI use
- Test for bias regularly
- Give people opt-out options
- Never use AI for high-stakes decisions without human oversight
- Develop an ethics framework

### Q: What about data privacy for our beneficiaries?

**A:** Critical considerations:

- Only collect necessary data (data minimization)
- Obtain informed consent
- Use secure, reputable vendors
- Never share PII with AI vendors unless absolutely necessary
- Anonymize data when possible
- Comply with GDPR, CCPA, and other regulations

## Fundraising

### Q: Can AI write our grant proposals?

**A:** AI can assist but shouldn't write grants from scratch. Best use:

- Draft initial content based on your notes
- Refine and improve existing drafts
- Generate compelling language
- Check for clarity and grammar
- Suggest data visualizations

**You still need:** Human insight, relationship context, authentic stories, and final review.

### Q: Will AI help us find major donors?

**A:** Yes! AI tools can:

- Analyze giving patterns to identify upgrade potential
- Screen donor lists for wealth indicators
- Predict optimal ask amounts
- Determine best contact timing
- Personalize outreach at scale

**But remember:** Relationships still matter. AI identifies prospects; humans build relationships.

## Technical Questions

### Q: What's the difference between AI and machine learning?

**A:** Machine Learning (ML) is a subset of AI. All ML is AI, but not all AI is ML:

- **AI** = Broad field of intelligent computer systems
- **ML** = Specific approach where systems learn from data
- **Deep Learning** = Advanced ML using neural networks

### Q: Do we need to hire a data scientist?

**A:** Probably not initially. Most modern AI tools don't require data science expertise. Consider hiring or consulting with a data scientist when you:

- Have large datasets requiring custom analysis
- Need to build proprietary models
- Want to integrate multiple AI systems
- Are scaling beyond off-the-shelf solutions

## Tools & Vendors

### Q: Which AI tools should we start with?

**A:** Based on your needs:

**For Content Creation:**
- ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini (Free/paid tiers)

**For Fundraising:**
- Bloomerang, DonorPerfect, or Little Green Light (with AI features)

**For Operations:**
- Zapier (automation), DocuSign (document processing)

**For Communications:**
- Canva (design), Grammarly (writing), DeepL (translation)

See our [Tools Directory](reference/tools.md) for comprehensive list.

### Q: How do we avoid vendor lock-in?

**A:** Smart strategies:

- Choose tools with data export options
- Avoid proprietary formats when possible
- Maintain backups of your data
- Build in-house expertise alongside vendor tools
- Review contracts for exit clauses

## Community & Culture

### Q: How do we maintain our organization's "human touch" with AI?

**A:** AI should enhance, not replace, human connection:

- Use AI for repetitive tasks, freeing staff for relationship-building
- Always have human oversight for sensitive communications
- Be transparent about what's automated vs. personal
- Design AI interactions to feel helpful, not impersonal
- Regularly gather feedback from your community

### Q: Can AI understand cultural nuance?

**A:** Current AI has limitations with cultural context:

- May miss subtleties in non-Western cultures
- Can perpetuate stereotypes from training data
- May not understand specific community terminology

**Best practices:**
- Always have humans from the community review AI outputs
- Test AI tools with your specific community
- Provide cultural context in prompts
- Use AI as a draft, not final product

## Future & Trends

### Q: Will AI replace non-profit jobs?

**A:** AI will transform jobs, not eliminate them:

- **Automated:** Repetitive administrative tasks
- **Enhanced:** Strategic planning, relationship building
- **Emerging:** AI trainers, ethics officers, automation specialists

Focus on developing skills AI can't replicate: empathy, creative problem-solving, community relationship building.

### Q: What AI developments should we watch?

**A:** Key trends for non-profits:

- More affordable, accessible tools
- Better multilingual capabilities
- Improved bias detection and fairness
- Stronger privacy protections
- Industry-specific AI solutions for non-profits

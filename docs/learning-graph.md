## How to Use the Learning Graph

The interactive visualization shows:

- **200 Concepts** as nodes
- **800+ Dependencies** as arrows
- **Color Coding** by Bloom's Taxonomy level
- **Filtering** by domain and level
- **Interactive Tooltips** with concept details

### Navigation Tips

1. **Drag nodes** to rearrange the layout
2. **Hover over nodes** to see concept details
3. **Use filters** to focus on specific domains or Bloom's levels
4. **Click nodes** to highlight their connections
5. **Export data** for offline analysis

### Understanding the Graph

**Node Colors:**
- 🟢 Green: Levels 1-2 (Remember/Understand)
- 🔵 Blue: Level 3 (Apply)
- 🟣 Purple: Level 4 (Analyze)
- 🔴 Pink: Levels 5-6 (Evaluate/Create)

**Arrows:** Show prerequisite relationships (learn A before B)

    Concept Enumeration: AI Integration for Non-Profits¶

    Complete List of 200 Core Concepts¶

    Format: ID | Concept Name | Category | Bloom's Level | Description¶

    CATEGORY 1: FOUNDATIONAL AI CONCEPTS (Concepts 1-30)¶

    001 | Artificial Intelligence (AI) | Foundation | 1 | Computer systems that can perform tasks typically requiring human intelligence

    002 | Machine Learning (ML) | Foundation | 1 | Subset of AI where systems learn from data without explicit programming

    003 | Deep Learning | Foundation | 1 | ML technique using neural networks with multiple layers

    004 | Natural Language Processing (NLP) | Foundation | 1 | AI's ability to understand and generate human language

    005 | Computer Vision | Foundation | 1 | AI's ability to interpret and understand visual information

    006 | Neural Network | Foundation | 2 | Computing system inspired by biological neural networks

    007 | Algorithm | Foundation | 1 | Step-by-step procedure for solving a problem or completing a task

    008 | Training Data | Foundation | 2 | Dataset used to teach an AI model to make predictions

    009 | Model | Foundation | 2 | Mathematical representation of a process learned from data

    010 | Supervised Learning | Foundation | 2 | ML approach where models learn from labeled training data

    011 | Unsupervised Learning | Foundation | 2 | ML approach where models find patterns in unlabeled data

    012 | Reinforcement Learning | Foundation | 2 | ML approach where agents learn through trial and error with rewards

    013 | Classification | Foundation | 2 | Task of categorizing data into predefined classes

    014 | Regression | Foundation | 2 | Predicting continuous numerical values based on input data

    015 | Clustering | Foundation | 2 | Grouping similar data points together without predefined categories

    016 | Feature | Foundation | 2 | Individual measurable property or characteristic of data

    017 | Prediction | Foundation | 2 | Forecasting future outcomes based on historical data

    018 | Inference | Foundation | 2 | Process of using a trained model to make predictions on new data

    019 | Overfitting | Foundation | 3 | When a model learns training data too well, including noise

    020 | Underfitting | Foundation | 3 | When a model is too simple to capture underlying data patterns

    021 | Accuracy | Foundation | 3 | Measure of how often a model makes correct predictions

    022 | Precision | Foundation | 3 | Proportion of positive predictions that are actually correct

    023 | Recall | Foundation | 3 | Proportion of actual positives correctly identified by the model

    024 | F1 Score | Foundation | 4 | Harmonic mean of precision and recall

    025 | Confusion Matrix | Foundation | 4 | Table showing prediction accuracy across different classes

    026 | ROC Curve | Foundation | 4 | Graph showing classifier performance across thresholds

    027 | Hyperparameter | Foundation | 3 | Configuration setting that controls the learning process

    028 | Optimization | Foundation | 3 | Process of finding the best model parameters

    029 | Gradient Descent | Foundation | 3 | Algorithm for finding minimum of a function iteratively

    030 | Cross-Validation | Foundation | 4 | Technique for assessing model performance on unseen data

    CATEGORY 2: GENERATIVE AI & LLMs (Concepts 31-50)¶

    031 | Generative AI | Gen AI | 2 | AI systems that create new content (text, images, audio, video)

    032 | Large Language Model (LLM) | Gen AI | 2 | AI model trained on vast text data to understand and generate language

    033 | GPT (Generative Pre-trained Transformer) | Gen AI | 2 | Type of LLM architecture developed by OpenAI

    034 | Prompt | Gen AI | 3 | Input text given to an AI model to generate a response

    035 | Prompt Engineering | Gen AI | 3 | Crafting effective prompts to get desired AI outputs

    036 | Token | Gen AI | 2 | Basic unit of text processed by language models

    037 | Context Window | Gen AI | 3 | Maximum amount of text an LLM can process at once

    038 | Temperature | Gen AI | 3 | Parameter controlling randomness in AI text generation

    039 | Fine-tuning | Gen AI | 4 | Adapting a pre-trained model to specific tasks or domains

    040 | Transfer Learning | Gen AI | 4 | Applying knowledge from one task to another related task

    041 | Zero-shot Learning | Gen AI | 4 | Model performing tasks without specific training examples

    042 | Few-shot Learning | Gen AI | 4 | Model learning from very few examples

    043 | Embeddings | Gen AI | 3 | Numerical representations of words or concepts in vector space

    044 | Transformer Architecture | Gen AI | 3 | Neural network design enabling parallel processing of sequences

    045 | Attention Mechanism | Gen AI | 3 | Model component that focuses on relevant parts of input

    046 | Hallucination | Gen AI | 3 | When AI generates plausible but incorrect or nonsensical information

    047 | Grounding | Gen AI | 4 | Connecting AI outputs to reliable, verifiable sources

    048 | RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) | Gen AI | 4 | Combining information retrieval with text generation

    049 | Multimodal AI | Gen AI | 3 | AI systems processing multiple types of data (text, image, audio)

    050 | AI Agent | Gen AI | 4 | Autonomous AI system that can plan and execute tasks

    CATEGORY 3: AI ETHICS & BIAS (Concepts 51-70)¶

    051 | AI Ethics | Ethics | 2 | Moral principles governing AI development and deployment

    052 | Algorithmic Bias | Ethics | 2 | Systematic errors in AI that create unfair outcomes

    053 | Dataset Bias | Ethics | 3 | Skewed training data leading to biased model outputs

    054 | Selection Bias | Ethics | 3 | Non-representative sampling in training data

    055 | Confirmation Bias | Ethics | 3 | AI reinforcing existing prejudices in data

    056 | Fairness | Ethics | 4 | Ensuring equitable treatment across different groups

    057 | Equity | Ethics | 4 | Providing resources based on individual needs for equal outcomes

    058 | Discrimination | Ethics | 2 | Unfair treatment based on protected characteristics

    059 | Transparency | Ethics | 4 | Openness about how AI systems work and make decisions

    060 | Explainability | Ethics | 4 | Ability to understand and interpret AI decisions

    061 | Accountability | Ethics | 5 | Clear responsibility for AI system outcomes

    062 | Black Box | Ethics | 3 | AI system whose decision-making process is opaque

    063 | Interpretability | Ethics | 4 | Degree to which humans can understand model behavior

    064 | Algorithmic Auditing | Ethics | 5 | Systematic examination of AI for bias and fairness

    065 | Disparate Impact | Ethics | 4 | Neutral policy with discriminatory effect on protected groups

    066 | Digital Divide | Ethics | 3 | Gap between those with and without access to technology

    067 | Informed Consent | Ethics | 3 | Agreement to AI use with full understanding of implications

    068 | Beneficence | Ethics | 4 | AI designed to benefit and do good for people

    069 | Non-maleficence | Ethics | 4 | AI principle of "do no harm"

    070 | Justice | Ethics | 5 | Fair distribution of AI benefits and burdens

    Chapter 9: AI for Content Creation and Communication¶

    Creating compelling content at scale.

    Learning Objectives: Leverage AI for writing, design, social media, and brand consistency

    Time: 3-4 hours

    9.1 The Content Challenge¶

    Non-profits need constant content: - Social media posts (daily) - Email newsletters (weekly) - Blog articles (monthly) - Grant proposals (ongoing) - Annual reports - Marketing materials

    Reality: Limited staff, limited time, unlimited needs

    AI Solution: Draft, edit, and optimize content faster

    9.2 AI Writing Tools¶

    Popular Platforms¶

    Tool Best For Cost
    ChatGPT General writing Free/Paid
    Claude Long-form, analysis Free/Paid
    Jasper Marketing copy Paid
    Copy.ai Quick social posts Free/Paid
    Grammarly Editing Free/Paid

    Use Cases¶

    Social Media: - Post ideas and captions - Hashtag suggestions - Engagement responses - Content calendars

    Email Marketing: - Newsletter articles - Subject lines - Personalized outreach - Donor updates

    Grant Writing: - Program descriptions - Need statements - Evaluation plans - Budget narratives

    9.3 Brand Voice Consistency¶

    Training AI on Your Voice¶

    Steps: 1. Collect 10-20 examples of your best content 2. Identify voice characteristics (warm, professional, inspiring) 3. Create style guide 4. Use examples in prompts 5. Iterate until consistent

    Example Prompt: 

    Write like this organization:
[paste 3 sample posts]

Key traits:
- Warm and inclusive
- Focus on community impact
- Use storytelling
- Avoid jargon
- Celebrate diversity

Now write a post about our volunteer program.

    9.4 AI Image Generation¶

    Tools¶

    • DALL-E (OpenAI) - High quality, versatile
    • Midjourney - Artistic, creative
    • Stable Diffusion - Open source
    • Adobe Firefly - Commercial safe
    • Canva AI - Integrated design

    Use Cases¶

    Social Media Graphics: - Event announcements - Quote cards - Infographics - Campaign visuals

    Marketing Materials: - Flyer backgrounds - Newsletter headers - Website images - Presentation slides

    Best Practices¶

    ✅ Use for backgrounds and abstract concepts
    ✅ Combine with real photos for authenticity
    ✅ Always review for appropriateness
    ✅ Check licensing terms
    ✅ Disclose AI-generated images

    ❌ Don't use for people (uncanny valley)
    ❌ Avoid for sensitive topics
    ❌ Don't misrepresent as real photos

    9.5 Video Content with AI¶

    Video Creation Tools¶

    • Descript - Edit video like text
    • Synthesia - AI avatars
    • Runway - AI video editing
    • Pictory - Text to video

    Applications¶

    Training Videos: - Staff onboarding - Volunteer orientation - Safety procedures - Software tutorials

    Marketing: - Impact stories - Event recaps - Program highlights - Testimonials

    9.6 Social Media Management¶

    AI-Powered Platforms¶

    • Buffer - Scheduling + AI suggestions
    • Hootsuite - Multi-platform management
    • Later - Visual planning
    • Sprout Social - Analytics + automation

    AI Features¶

    Content Optimization: - Best posting times - Hashtag recommendations - Caption improvements - A/B testing

    Engagement: - Response suggestions - Sentiment monitoring - Trend identification - Influencer discovery

    9.7 Practical Workflow¶

    Weekly Content Creation¶

    Monday: Plan - AI suggests content themes - Review analytics for what worked - Create content calendar

    Tuesday-Thursday: Create - AI drafts posts - Human edits and personalizes - Design graphics with AI assistance - Schedule posts

    Friday: Engage - Monitor comments - AI suggests responses - Thank engagers - Share user content

    Chapter Summary¶

    • AI accelerates content creation
    • Maintain brand voice through training
    • Image generation for visual content
    • Video tools simplify production
    • Social media AI optimizes timing
    • Always human-review AI outputs
    • Focus AI on drafting, humans on strategy
